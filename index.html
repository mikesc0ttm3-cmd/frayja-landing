Frayja: Privacy-First Messaging & File Transfer

Vision & Purpose

Frayja is built on the conviction that privacy is a fundamental human right...

  • Sovereignty by Design: Decentralized PKI + IPFS, no central servers.
  • Privacy for All: No phone numbers, no cloud sync, no metadata exposure.
  • Intuitive Security: Advanced cryptography made simple for everyone.
  • Mythic Branding & Community: Empowerment through storytelling and design.
  • Ethical Monetization: Paid licenses, enterprise deployments, no ads.
  • Global Impact: Serving NGOs, human rights orgs, enterprises, and individuals.
