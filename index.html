Sovereign, privacy-first messaging and file transfer for the post-cloud era.

Why Frayja Exists

We live in a post-cloud era where privacy is no longer optional—it’s essential. Centralised messaging platforms and email services have repeatedly failed to protect user data:

  • WhatsApp breach (2025): Over 3.5 billion phone numbers and profile data exposed due to weak rate-limiting and scraping vulnerabilities. #[Source]
  • TeleMessage hack: Signal and WhatsApp clones used by US officials were compromised, exposing archived messages and credentials.
  • Email vulnerabilities: SMTP still negotiates encryption in plaintext, making interception possible. Gmail and Yahoo have faced breaches and continue scanning inboxes for ads. #[Source]

Frayja’s Purpose

  • End-to-End PKI Encryption: Only intended recipients can decrypt messages.
  • User-Held Keys: Private keys never leave your device.
  • IPFS-Based Delivery: No central servers, no metadata leakage.
  • Desktop-Only: Maximum control, no OS-level surveillance.
  • Zero Third-Party Dependence: No reliance on proprietary infrastructure.
