About Frayja

Our Mission

Frayja was founded on the belief that privacy is a fundamental human right. We empower users with tools that eliminate metadata exposure and restore digital sovereignty.

Our Approach

  • Decentralized architecture using PKI and IPFS.
  • Desktop-first design to avoid mobile OS surveillance.
  • Privacy-preserving payments via Polygon and Rune Tokens.

Who We Serve

Privacy advocates, journalists, NGOs, regulated industries, and individuals who refuse to compromise on confidentiality.

Our Story

Frayja is developed by Instanton Ltd (NZ), blending technical innovation with mythic branding to build a community committed to intentional communication.
